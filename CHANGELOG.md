# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (9 November 2026)

First version, written from the run of 9 November 2026 on InWove.

- `PROTOCOL.md`: principles, roles, thirteen phases, the files, the gates, the sizes, and what went wrong.
- `protocol.json`: the knobs as data, each with its reason.
- `templates/`: the reader, builder and lead briefs, the report format, the build plan, one build brief, the
  findings file, the notes file, and the prompts for readers, builders, the facts writer and five kinds of lead.
- `scripts/`: `make_manifests.py`, `resplit.py`, `check_coverage.py`, `citecheck.py`, `render_run.py`, with tests.
- `examples/inwove/`: the run's sources, its slices, and a short account of it.
- `facts/interop-facts.md`: the run's public facts page.
